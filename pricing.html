Common questions

What happens if I run out of credits?+
AI-powered operations will pause until you top up. Your projects, code, and data remain fully accessible — only new AI operations are paused. You can top up instantly in the app or enable auto top-up to avoid interruption.
Do unused credits roll over?+
Monthly plan credits reset at the start of each billing cycle and don't roll over. Purchased top-up credits are valid for 30 days from the date of purchase.
Can I switch plans at any time?+
Yes — you can upgrade at any time and the change takes effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Annual subscribers can upgrade mid-year and we'll prorate the difference.
What's the minimum commitment for Business and Enterprise?+
Business requires a minimum of 5 seats and Enterprise a minimum of 25 seats. Both are available on monthly or annual billing. Enterprise plans can be customised — talk to our sales team for bespoke arrangements.
Is my code and data secure?+
Yes. Brunelly connects to your existing repositories — we never store your source code on our servers. Enterprise plans include options for dedicated infrastructure, on-premise deployment, and compliance controls including SOC2 and GDPR.
Can I try Brunelly before committing to a paid plan?+
Absolutely — the Starter plan is free forever with no credit card required. Connect your repo, explore the platform, and upgrade when you're ready. You can also book a live demo with our team to see Brunelly working on a real codebase.
What integrations does Brunelly support?+
Brunelly integrates with GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps, and Bitbucket — no migration required. Enterprise plans include RESTful API and webhook support for custom integrations with your existing toolchain.
